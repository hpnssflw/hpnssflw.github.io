For five weeks my research agent's config listed four sources I never read: `reddit`, `rss`, `releases`, `web_search` sat in its topic YAMLs while the code only knew Hacker News.

## What to watch for

- Lookup ran the wrong direction: the run loop iterates its own `CONNECTORS` dict and skips anything not listed in a topic's `sources` — never the reverse. A key with no connector is invisible, not an error.
- The loader passed `sources` through as a raw dict — no schema, no "unknown key" check. Whatever I typed there was accepted and quietly ignored.
- Public copy repeated the config's claims: `/researcher/agent/` still describes "six kinds of source," though only two are wired up.

## Why it's interesting

- The fix is cheap: reject unknown source keys at load time, so a stray key fails loudly instead of doing nothing for weeks. I haven't made that change — that's the direction to look.

## In practice

[`agent/main.py`](https://github.com/hpnssflw/hpnssflw.github.io/blob/main/agent/main.py)'s `CONNECTORS` held only `hacker_news` from the scaffold until commit `8d8033c` dropped the dead keys from the topic YAMLs; [`agent/config.py`](https://github.com/hpnssflw/hpnssflw.github.io/blob/main/agent/config.py) is where the validation belongs. What caught it wasn't a test — it was writing the Content Direction roadmap, which required rereading `CONNECTORS` and surfaced the mismatch.
