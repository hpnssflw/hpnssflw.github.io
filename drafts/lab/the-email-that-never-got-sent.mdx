SMTP delivery for the research agent was fully built — and never sent a single message.

## What to watch for

- "Built" isn't "working": subject/body assembly and the send call
  shipped, but SMTP credentials were never configured — not in
  `agent/.env`, not as repo secrets — so every attempt failed, caught
  and logged, not delivered.
- Stale docs keep dead decisions alive: "add SMTP credentials, send a
  live email" stayed written down as the next step long after it was
  dropped, so it kept resurfacing as if still live (author's notes).
- A visible counter isn't action: the dashboard showed the pending
  count early on, but it still climbed past a hundred over a month —
  only a new channel cleared it, not the visibility.

## Why it's interesting

- I picked the channel before there was a reader to deliver to —
  twice: once for an on-page dashboard, once for Telegram — and
  neither pivot touched the queue's own logic.
- Decoupling collection/ranking from delivery made the channel
  swappable: SMTP became Telegram by rewriting two files in place, not
  restructuring the pipeline.

## In practice

[`agent/pending.py`](https://github.com/hpnssflw/hpnssflw.github.io/blob/main/agent/pending.py)
survived both pivots with its logic untouched — the Telegram rewrite
only reworded its docstrings. Delivery moved from SMTP to Telegram in
[`agent/deliver.py`](https://github.com/hpnssflw/hpnssflw.github.io/blob/main/agent/deliver.py) —
same queue, different channel.
